# DefiPrime

> Independent research on decentralized finance. On-chain analysis of protocols, exploits and market structure, plus curated directories of live DeFi products by category. Published since 2019.

Analysis pieces are written from primary sources: transaction hashes, block heights, contract state read at a pinned block, and protocol documentation. Identifiers are cited inline so every claim can be checked against the chain. Directory pages list live protocols in a category and are maintained by hand.
