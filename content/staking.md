Staking commits tokens to a proof-of-stake network in return for a share of issuance and fees, with the stake slashable if the validator misbehaves. Liquid staking issues a receipt token that stays tradable and usable as collateral while the deposit stays bonded, and restaking lends the same security to other services.

The filters separate liquid staking, restaking, validator operators that run nodes on behalf of delegators, and Bitcoin staking. Entries are ordered by value locked on DefiLlama, with featured listings first.
