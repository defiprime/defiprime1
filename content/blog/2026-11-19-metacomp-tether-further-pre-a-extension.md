On November 19, 2026, MetaComp announced an approximately US$10 million investment from Tether and Further Asset Management, extending its Pre-A+ financing. The Singapore payments company said the extension brings its Pre-A funding to about US$45 million, with the proceeds intended to fund licences, compliance infrastructure and additional payment channels.

The [company's announcement](https://www.mce.sg/metacomp-tether-further-pre-a-plus-investment/) describes the financing as an extension of the Pre-A+ round. It follows US$35 million in Pre-A and Pre-A+ funding completed in March 2026, backed by investors including Alibaba, Eastern Bell Capital, Noah, Sky9 Capital and Spark Venture. MetaComp already operates a fiat-to-stablecoin payments business. Its public product pages describe the conversion, collection and settlement services available to customers.

## Capital for existing payment corridors

MetaComp is directing the investment toward its Asia, Middle East and Africa corridors. The company says it will use the proceeds to strengthen its regulatory capabilities and improve compliance infrastructure, as well as develop additional payment channels in priority markets. According to the release, its established Asian clients are expanding into other Asian markets, the Middle East, Africa and Latin America.

Further is an Abu Dhabi-based investment platform focused on financial infrastructure and digital assets. According to the announcement, it will bring institutional relationships and sector expertise to MetaComp's Middle East expansion. Further managing partner Faisal Al Hammadi described the banking connection behind the investment: “For stablecoins to become everyday financial infrastructure, businesses need reliable connections to the banking systems they already use.”

Tether chief executive Paolo Ardoino tied the investment to emerging-market settlement. “Our investment in MetaComp reflects our focus on building that infrastructure in emerging markets, where faster and cheaper settlement matters most.”

MetaComp says its hybrid fiat and stablecoin infrastructure reduces settlement on these corridors from 2-7 days to within 24 hours by reducing intermediaries and fragmented networks. The settlement times are the company's figures from the financing announcement. Its product materials describe how it chooses between conventional dollar payments and stablecoin settlement for a transfer.

## How the payment products move funds

The [StableX engine product page](https://www.mce.sg/stablex-network/stablex/) describes a routing engine that selects a stablecoin or United States dollar rail according to cost, speed and settlement certainty. StableX supports always-on foreign exchange transactions using assets including $USDT and $USDC across major and exotic currencies. The engine is integrated into CAMP, MetaComp's institutional platform, with multi-currency support and liquidity aggregation.

The page includes a currency converter, but its displayed exchange rates carry a qualification: “All rates are indicative, non-binding, and subject to prevailing market conditions at execution.” A treasury team can use the interface to inspect a conversion indication; the published number is not a committed execution price.

The [PayX product page](https://www.mce.sg/camp-solutions/payx/) describes the service's on-ramp and off-ramp functions. It lists local collection and payout accounts for funding and transfers, with exchange access through CAMP. Supported digital assets include $USDT, $USDC, $FDUSD and $PYUSD, as well as $BTC and $ETH. Customers can use this existing service to convert between fiat money and the listed assets used on public chains.

PayX also offers payment on behalf of and collection on behalf of customers. The company describes individuals or businesses using digital assets or fiat to pay expenses such as electricity bills and salaries. The new capital is intended to extend these payment functions through additional channels. The financing announcement sets out no new customer price schedule.

## Reported scale and the entities behind it

MetaComp reports connections to more than 60 financial institutions and more than 170 payment service providers. It says the platform serves more than 1,000 institutional clients, supports 60 currencies across fiat and stablecoins, and processes more than US$1 billion in monthly volume. Assets under management exceed US$600 million. The release dates those figures to October 30, 2026; they are company-reported operating measures, not on-chain readings.

MetaComp describes itself as a Major Payment Institution licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. The release distinguishes its services from those provided by its affiliates. WealthX products and services are offered by affiliated licensed entities within their respective licence scopes. Securities and capital-markets products are offered and operated solely by Alpha Ladder Finance, according to the release's footnote. The release reports the wealth-management figure within this business description of MetaComp and its affiliated entities.

The company also operates the StableX Agentic Network, which uses stablecoins for transactions between AI agents. Network admission relies on its Know-Your-Agent compliance standard, while VisionX monitors fiat and on-chain activity. MetaComp says the network launched in November 2025 and has grown to more than 1,000 members reaching more than 100,000 users. Those network members and users are reported separately from the platform's institutional-client count.

Through AgentX, AI agents can access MetaComp's regulated payment capabilities directly. The announcement dates the launch of its agent-governance framework to April 2026. AgentX gives agents access to the company's payments infrastructure, which institutions also use through the CAMP platform and PayX services.

## Access and counterparty limits remain

MetaComp's [digital-payment-token risk disclosure](https://www.mce.sg/disclosure/) warns that its MAS licence does not ensure customers recover money or tokens if the provider or a relevant third party fails. It also states that MAS does not supervise the provider's unregulated services. Customers are told to understand how a token is created, transferred and held before transacting.

The same disclosure restricts service to persons incorporated in the People's Republic of China and persons using funds originating there, while excluding Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan from its defined restricted territory. It reserves MetaComp's discretion to suspend or revoke access where providing services may violate the applicable jurisdiction's laws. The expansion language in the funding release leaves these published access restrictions in place.

The announcement does not give a valuation, an investor-by-investor allocation of the approximately US$10 million, or a timetable naming which additional payment channels will open.
