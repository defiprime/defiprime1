On October 15, 2026, bStocks [reported](https://x.com/bstocksfinance/status/2110718961075757266) that its tokenized equities had crossed $1 billion in assets under management four months after their June launch. The company also said users had converted more than $6 billion between bStocks and the corresponding stocks on Binance.

The milestone is a company-reported figure rather than an on-chain reading. The announcement does not identify a chain, contract address, block or account set from which an outside reader could reproduce the total. It does explain what the figure covers, how conversions work and where users have been active.

bStocks describes its products as tokenized securities that provide one-to-one exposure to selected stocks and exchange-traded funds. They are not shares in the underlying companies. The product disclaimer says holders do not directly own the corresponding stock and have no affiliation with its issuer.

The tokens are offered under an approved prospectus in the Abu Dhabi Global Market. The company says they are available only to eligible users in permitted jurisdictions on a secondary-market basis. They are not offered to U.S. persons or from within the United States.

## Conversions connect tokens with direct stocks

The conversion mechanism is the main link between the token and Binance's direct stock product. Holders can convert a bStock into the corresponding stock at a one-to-one rate without a conversion fee. They can also move in the other direction. The company contrasts this with products that require a token sale followed by a separate stock purchase.

The reported conversion volume is more than six times the current assets under management. That does not mean $6 billion remains in the product. It measures cumulative movement between the two formats through October 2026, while the $1 billion figure describes assets under management at the reporting date.

For users, the mechanism creates a choice between the token and direct stock formats without requiring two market trades. The announcement says users can hold bStocks to trade outside regular hours, withdraw them to a self-custody wallet or use them in on-chain applications. A user can convert back when direct stock ownership inside Binance is preferred.

Coverage has expanded alongside the reported asset total. bStocks launched with five tickers in June and now supports nearly 100 tickers across areas including semiconductors, artificial-intelligence infrastructure and sector exchange-traded funds. The announcement does not list every supported instrument in the post.

The existing customer base also overlaps with Binance's other markets. The company says 58.5% of early bStocks users had traded perpetual futures, direct equities or both before their first bStock purchase as of August 2026. The figure points to adoption by customers already using leveraged or direct stock products, rather than a wholly separate group entering through tokenized equities.

Binance Research places that activity inside a tokenized-equity market whose capitalization rose 390.4% in 2026 through the announcement date. bStocks says it represented about one quarter of the sector's market capitalization in October. Both figures come from Binance's own research, so they provide company-sourced market context rather than an independent market tally.

The legal boundary matters after withdrawal. The disclaimer says tokenized securities may be held and transferred on-chain outside the central securities depository environment. Returning them to that environment is conditional. A holder may be unable to trade, redeem or otherwise use the securities inside the depository environment if those conditions are not met.

## Most trading occurs outside the regular session

The company says only 42% of bStocks volume occurred during regular U.S. market hours in August 2026. The remaining 58% was distributed across pre-market, post-market, overnight and weekend sessions. For comparison, the same announcement puts the regular-session share for conventional U.S. equities at 89.2%.

This distribution shows how customers are using the token format. Regular U.S. hours account for a minority of reported bStocks volume even though the reference assets are stocks and exchange-traded funds. Binance Research [described](https://x.com/BinanceResearch/status/2090963587833495953) trading as distributed across the full week rather than concentrated around the U.S. session.

Round-the-clock access changes when holders can react, but it does not remove product restrictions. Binance says it may restrict, suspend, reject, cancel or unwind access or transactions where it determines that activity may breach applicable law, sanctions requirements, product restrictions or eligibility rules. Availability therefore depends on both the trading schedule and the user's jurisdiction and status.

## On-chain use is growing from a small base

The announcement reports an 11.2% utilization rate for bStocks, which it says is about 3.8 percentage points above the tokenized-equity market overall. Binance Research separately [said](https://x.com/BinanceResearch/status/2105997126102831162) that bStocks had led the market on this measure since July 31. The research post defines the next benchmark as usage rather than issuance.

Borrowing provides one view of that usage. Outstanding borrowing against bStocks rose from 5.5% of deposited collateral at the end of June to 46.2% by September 10, 2026. The company put the latter position at about $3.1 million borrowed against $6.8 million of collateral. Those balances remain small relative to the reported $1 billion under management, so the figures describe a developing collateral market rather than broad use of the full asset base.

The wider transfer data moved faster. The announcement says on-chain transfers of tokenized stocks, including trading volume, exceeded $100 billion in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $6 billion in the first quarter. It attributes more than 90% of weekly transfer volume to bStocks at an early-August peak. These are Binance Research figures, not independently reproduced readings in the announcement.

The product's next test is whether on-chain borrowing and utilization continue to rise as assets under management grows. The announcement leaves open how much of the reported $1 billion is held in self-custody, used as collateral or kept inside Binance for conversion and trading.
