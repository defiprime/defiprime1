On October 14, 2026, DriveWealth and Ripple [announced an integration](https://ripple.com/ripple-press/drivewealth-and-ripple-partner-to-bring-institutional-digital-asset-infrastructure-to-global-investing/) covering stablecoin settlement, cross-border payments, custody and equity financing. DriveWealth will begin the settlement work in Europe with $RLUSD on the $XRP Ledger, using Ripple's mint and burn capabilities. The companies plan to extend that settlement setup to the United States.

The partnership puts stablecoin funding behind DriveWealth's brokerage infrastructure rather than launching a separate retail product. DriveWealth supplies brokerage systems to more than 100 partners worldwide, including fintechs, digital wallets and financial institutions that offer access to American markets. Ripple says its regulatory footprint spans more than 85 licenses.

The immediate mismatch is between trading and funding hours. American equities settle on T+1, while international dollar transfers can take longer. DriveWealth already supports trading 24 hours a day on five weekdays. Its chief executive, Naureen Hassan, said, "We've already enabled 24/5 trading; now we need the funding to match, so clients can move with the markets instead of waiting for them."

## How the integration is divided

Stablecoin settlement starts in Europe. DriveWealth will use Ripple's stablecoin infrastructure, $RLUSD and the $XRP Ledger. Ripple's mint and burn functions will connect token issuance and redemption to the settlement flow. The announcement gives no production date for this European integration and describes American expansion as a later plan.

DriveWealth is taking the same regional order with custody. The companies say the first custody work is in Europe and that additional markets will follow. The release does not tie custody expansion to the American settlement plan, so partners cannot assume both products reach each market together. Each product has its own stated scope: settlement uses $RLUSD and XRPL, payments receive cross-border funds, and custody covers DriveWealth's digital asset needs.

A separate Ripple Payments integration will accept cross-border funding from DriveWealth's global partners. This is the inbound route into the brokerage platform. The companies present it as a way to reduce dependence on transfer windows and bank cutoff times when a partner needs to fund activity in another market.

Custody is also starting in Europe. DriveWealth is working with Ripple on digital asset custody there, with more markets planned over time. DriveWealth will move treasury and custody infrastructure onto Ripple's rails. The stated goal is one real-time view across dollars, stablecoins and crypto, with transfers between those balances.

The equity-financing component is less settled. DriveWealth and Ripple Prime are exploring opportunities through Ripple Prime's Delta One business, which covers American equities and equity derivatives. The announcement does not describe a product, customer, collateral schedule or start date for that work. Operators should distinguish this exploratory component from the announced settlement, payments and custody integrations.

## What changes for brokerage funding

DriveWealth clients will be able to fund and manage collateral accounts with stablecoins that convert to cash in near real time. The operational benefit is narrower than continuous equity settlement. A client can move funding without waiting for a bank wire, but the release does not say that the underlying securities settlement cycle changes from T+1.

That distinction matters for treasury teams. The integration targets the cash leg before and around a trade. It can shorten the time between a partner sending value and DriveWealth recognizing usable cash. It also gives DriveWealth a common system for viewing treasury and custody balances in dollars, stablecoins and crypto. The announcement does not identify supported partner jurisdictions, conversion spreads, transaction fees or service-level commitments.

The integration also separates the funding asset from the cash balance used inside the brokerage account. The release says clients can send stablecoins and convert them to cash in near real time. It does not say that equities will settle in $RLUSD or that customers will hold tokenized shares on XRPL. For a partner integrating the system, the relevant handoff is therefore stablecoin to cash, followed by the existing brokerage process.

DriveWealth's role is broader than a single trading front end. Its infrastructure lets fintechs, wallets and financial institutions provide access to American markets. A successful deployment could put the same Ripple funding route behind several partner products. The release does not name which of DriveWealth's more than 100 partners will adopt it first, so the initial distribution cannot yet be measured.

International partners are the clearest initial users. They currently plan funding around cross-border transfer windows and cutoff times. Ripple's system is meant to let them send stablecoins into DriveWealth and convert those balances to cash near the time the funds are needed. The later American settlement expansion would add a domestic endpoint, although the companies have not provided its timing.

Hassan said the aim is for capital to keep working overnight and on weekends rather than remain idle. The release also says DriveWealth will extend the funding and custody experience its clients already use to stablecoins and digital assets. That framing leaves DriveWealth as the brokerage layer while Ripple provides the stablecoin, payment and custody infrastructure underneath it.

## What remains open

The announcement identifies Europe as the first settlement and custody market but does not name a launch date or the European entities and partners that will use the integrations first. It also leaves the American expansion schedule, fees, conversion terms and eligible currencies unspecified. Those details will determine whether partners use $RLUSD for routine brokerage funding or only for transfers that do not fit bank operating hours.

The next dated milestone is October 28, 2026, when the companies are scheduled to discuss the partnership in a fireside chat at Ripple Swell in New York City. The unresolved issue is whether that session will add deployment dates and commercial terms for the European launch and planned American expansion.
