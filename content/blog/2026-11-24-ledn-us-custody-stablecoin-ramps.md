On November 24, 2026, Ledn executives described a United States custody account combining purchases, swaps and borrowing in [statements published by The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-24-ledn-launches-us-custody-account-bitcoin-gold-stablecoins-lending-445411). The account's live documentation shows how clients can move between domestic bank wires and Ethereum-based stablecoins and tokenized gold, alongside bitcoin. BitGo Bank & Trust, N.A. holds the assets.

Customer access preceded the publication date. Ledn's [product guide](https://help.ledn.io/hc/en-us/articles/44855809017623-What-is-a-Ledn-Custody-account) says existing clients would receive invitations from November 16, 2026. Its [United States terms](https://www.ledn.io/legal/terms-of-service-us) have the same effective date and name Ledn US LLC, a Delaware company, as the counterparty. The release cited by The Block was not available in the sources reviewed.

“Most people buy it in one place, keep it in another, and when they need cash, they sell it. Now they can do all three in one account,” co-founder and chief strategy officer Mauricio Di Bartolomeo said in the statement quoted by The Block. The custody documentation sets separate rules for account access and loan eligibility.

## Bank wires in, Ethereum tokens out

The account holds bitcoin, Tether Gold ($XAUt), US dollars and the stablecoins $USDT, $USDC and $USAT. Customers can deposit digital assets, trade among supported assets and withdraw to their own wallets. Ledn says BitGo holds the assets for clients' benefit, separately from its own assets. The assets are never lent out to generate interest.

Ledn's [deposit instructions](https://help.ledn.io/hc/en-us/articles/44855944120727-How-do-I-link-my-bank-or-wallet-and-deposit-funds-assets) specify Ethereum ERC-20 transfers for the gold token and supported stablecoins. Bitcoin uses the Bitcoin network. Deposits appear after network confirmation. The instructions warn that sending assets over an unsupported network may permanently lose them, and the withdrawal guide carries the same Ethereum restriction.

To deposit dollars, clients link a United States bank account in their own name and follow the wire instructions inside the app. The reference attached to the wire lets Ledn match the incoming funds to the custody account. Ledn says there is no deposit limit and charges no wire deposit fee, although the sending bank may charge its own fee.

Withdrawing dollars requires bank approval. The [withdrawal guide](https://help.ledn.io/hc/en-us/articles/44855971382295-How-do-I-make-withdrawals-from-my-Ledn-Custody-account) allows up to three linked bank accounts, all in the client's name. Each bank account must pass a check before it can receive a withdrawal, usually within 48 hours. The guide permits domestic dollar withdrawals; international wires are unavailable.

## No commission still leaves a spread

Ledn's [trading guide](https://help.ledn.io/hc/en-us/articles/44855933295511-How-do-I-buy-sell-or-trade-in-my-Ledn-Custody-account) says trades have no platform or transaction fees. The cost sits in the quote: trades between the supported dollar stablecoins carry a 0.1% spread, and all other trades carry a 0.5% spread. That includes purchases and sales using US dollars.

BitGo executes trades at its own price. According to the guide, the spread included in the price shown to the customer is Ledn's compensation. Inside Ledn, a client chooses the assets to trade, enters an amount, reviews the quote and confirms it. The resulting assets appear in the custody account.

Each trade must be worth at least $50 and no more than $3 million. The daily trading limit is $10 million. Those limits apply to trading, while dollar deposits have no limit. Confirmation is final: the trading guide says clients cannot reverse or cancel a trade after confirming it.

## Migration changes the account, not existing loan terms

Existing clients who accept BitGo's agreements have their Transaction Account balances moved automatically into custody. Their Transaction Account then closes, though its history remains available. Ledn's [migration instructions](https://help.ledn.io/hc/en-us/articles/44855925994263-How-are-my-assets-held-in-the-Ledn-Custody-account) say existing loans keep their rate, term and collateral. Clients who do not set up custody can continue using their existing Transaction Account and loans as before.

After migration, loan top-ups, including Auto Top-Up, draw from the custody balance. Remaining collateral returns to the custody account when a loan closes. New United States clients open with custody from the start. Transaction Accounts are no longer available to them.

Individual clients can access custody in all United States states, including states where Ledn loans are unavailable. The migration guide excludes California, Connecticut, Hawaii, Nevada, North Dakota, South Dakota, Tennessee, Washington and Washington D.C. from Dollar Loan availability. Clients in those locations can hold supported tokens in custody but remain ineligible for those loans.

## Ownership and settlement follow different agreements

The United States terms set out who owns and holds the assets in each account. Digital assets deposited into a Transaction Account become Ledn US's legal and beneficial property. A custody customer enters BitGo's Custodial Services Agreement and Electronic Trading Agreement directly. Ledn US is not a party to those agreements and does not hold the custody account's assets.

BitGo may act on instructions from Ledn as though they came from the customer, without inquiry. Under the terms, BitGo has no obligation to check whether a client authorized a Ledn instruction. It also has no obligation to verify whether the instruction is permitted by the client's loan agreement or other agreements with Ledn.

Settlement can delay access to the proceeds of a custody trade. BitGo's settlement process may run only on business days and at set times. Until settlement, purchased digital assets or dollars may be unavailable for withdrawal or transfer. The terms also warn that a trade can fail to settle.

Conversion to custody is one-way. Once an amount equal to the Transaction Account balance moves into custody, Ledn has satisfied its contractual obligation to return that balance. Addresses previously safelisted for the Transaction Account do not carry over to the custody account.

## Withdrawals can face new address checks

BitGo places a hold on withdrawals above $5,000 to an address that has never received a withdrawal from Ledn Custody. An address used with Ledn before migration still faces that check. The guide says clients can first send $5,000 or less to an address. Once that initial transfer has been sent, later, larger withdrawals to the same destination avoid the first-time address hold.

Withdrawals above $500,000 require additional verification steps. The withdrawal guide warns that digital asset transfers are irreversible and tells customers to check both the destination address and the network before sending. These address and verification checks apply alongside the network restrictions in the deposit and withdrawal instructions.

Ledn says accounts for businesses and clients outside the United States will follow later. The product guide gives no opening date for either group.
