On October 13, Neoprime Capital [announced $NEOPRIME](https://x.com/NeoprimeCapital/status/2110051816704811269), a Canton-native investment vehicle that it says is backed by shares in pre-IPO companies. Hecto developed the product, and the token is to be issued on Canton. The announcement said trading is coming soon but gave no opening date.

The launch gives Hecto's private-market product line a named asset and a network. It does not yet give prospective holders the final documents needed to evaluate the claim on the underlying shares. Hecto's [PMX product page](https://www.hecto.inc/products/pmx) says product documents define exposure mechanics, rights, restrictions, and updates. Access also depends on product rules, onboarding, and jurisdiction.

## What Hecto has announced

Hecto describes PMX as a tokenized route to economic exposure to private-market assets. The company says $NEOPRIME is backed by real shares, not merely a reference price or prediction market. Its Canton Forum introduction calls the token Hecto's first pre-IPO token on Canton and says one token is backed by multiple pre-IPO companies.

The [forum introduction](https://forum.canton.network/t/neoprime-canton-s-first-pre-ipo-rwa-token-launches/9300) names Anduril and Shield AI as defense businesses in the product theme. It also names Prometheus and Figure AI under advanced manufacturing and robotics, and Erebor Bank among the financial companies serving that theme. These names describe the announced exposure universe. Hecto's general PMX page warns that company references are illustrative, subject to final product documents, and do not imply direct equity rights or guaranteed availability.

That distinction matters for anyone comparing $NEOPRIME with owning shares directly. Hecto says PMX provides economic exposure through a tokenized product route. It separately says holder rights and claims are documented at the product level. A token described as share-backed therefore does not, on the current public material alone, establish that each holder becomes a direct shareholder of every named company.

Hecto also says eligibility, issuance, transferability, and product documentation are part of the design from the start. Transfer rules can differ by product, venue, jurisdiction, and user eligibility. The company says issuer structure, product documents, and restrictions are defined before distribution. Those controls make the final terms operationally important, since a holder's ability to acquire or transfer the token may depend on more than possession of a Canton wallet.

## How issuance and pricing are meant to work

The PMX page describes flexible issuance, with supply able to respond to demand under the product terms. It also says mint or redeem mechanics can support pricing discipline. Hecto has not presented those mechanics as universal promises. Both statements are qualified by the product terms, which have not been included in the $NEOPRIME launch announcement.

For a holder, issuance and redemption are separate questions. Flexible issuance describes how token supply may expand under the terms. Mint and redeem mechanics describe the route that may connect token supply with the product's private-market exposure. Hecto says those mechanics can support pricing discipline, but the published PMX page does not say every holder will be eligible to use both routes. Eligibility, jurisdiction, onboarding, and the final product rules remain part of access.

The distinction also affects how users should read a secondary-market price. Cantex can provide a venue for transfers under the product structure. The product documents still determine holder rights and claims, while venue, jurisdiction, and user eligibility can determine transferability. A market quote therefore cannot answer what legal claim the token carries or which users can access issuance and redemption. Those answers need to come from the product documents Hecto says will define the mechanics and restrictions.

Secondary-market rules are likewise set by the product structure. Cantex [said on October 13 at 17:33 UTC](https://x.com/Cantex_io/status/2110061419072766342) that $NEOPRIME trading would arrive on its venue soon. The exchange did not publish a listing time in that statement. Until trading and transfer rules are live, users cannot infer liquidity, spreads, redemption access, or the relationship between token supply and the private shares from the announcement alone.

Hecto's stated access process begins with a waitlist. The company then reviews eligibility, jurisdiction, onboarding, and the applicable product rules. Eligible users receive updates according to timing, documentation, and product availability. This makes the current launch an announcement of the product and intended venue rather than evidence that unrestricted acquisition or redemption is available now.

The structure differs from a static watchlist. Hecto says PMX is designed around defined issuance, product rules, pricing discipline, and documented holder protections. It also says creditor protections are set at the product level. For allocators, the useful comparison will be between the final legal claim and the market price, not simply between $NEOPRIME and estimated valuations of the named companies.

## What users still need

The next documents need to identify the issuer, the custodian or legal owner of the underlying shares, and the rights attached to $NEOPRIME. They also need to show which named companies are actually included at launch, how exposure is weighted, how net asset value is calculated, and whether holders can mint or redeem. The current materials define these matters as product-level terms without publishing the $NEOPRIME-specific answers.

Transfer eligibility is the other practical constraint. Hecto says onboarding and jurisdiction apply, while transferability can vary by venue and user eligibility. A secondary listing on Cantex would create a trading venue, but it would not remove the restrictions written into the product structure. Prospective users should therefore treat the forthcoming product documents and venue rules as part of the asset, not as paperwork separate from it.

Hecto says $NEOPRIME is the first of multiple planned pre-IPO tokens, each with a distinct market theme. The immediate milestone is the promised Cantex trading launch. The open issue is whether the final documents connect the token, the underlying shares, holder claims, and mint or redemption mechanics closely enough for users to verify the backing that the announcement describes.
