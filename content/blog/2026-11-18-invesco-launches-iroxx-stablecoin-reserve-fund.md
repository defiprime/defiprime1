Invesco [launched the Invesco Stablecoin Reserves Onchain Fund](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/invesco-launches-the-invesco-stablecoin-reserves-onchain-fund-bringing-invescos-established-liquidity-management-onchain-302941734.html) on November 17, 2026, giving institutional investors a tokenized money market fund intended to back payment stablecoins. Its shares, represented by $IROXX on Ethereum, are available through Superstate's infrastructure with a $1,000,000 minimum investment.

The fund is a registered government money market fund within Invesco Short-Term Investments Trust. Invesco manages its investments. Superstate provides the blockchain-integrated transfer agency and recordkeeping, with stablecoin issuers and digital asset infrastructure providers among the intended initial investors.

Invesco calls the offering GENIUS Act-compliant. The portfolio holds eligible reserve assets, not stablecoins or equity in stablecoin issuers. An issuer can hold these professionally managed reserves through tokenized fund shares and subscribe or redeem in dollars or $USDC.

## What the reserve fund holds

The [November 13 prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/205007/000119312526460554/d839618d497.htm) specifies cash and Treasury bills, notes and bonds with a remaining maturity of 93 days or less, or issued with a maturity of 93 days or less. It also permits overnight repurchase agreements collateralized by Treasuries and/or cash under the applicable legislation. Those holdings back the fund shares that stablecoin issuers would use as reserves.

As a Rule 2a-7 government money market fund, $IROXX must invest at least 99.5% of total assets in cash, government securities and repurchase agreements collateralized by cash or government securities. The fund targets a stable $1.00 share price. It values its holdings using amortized cost and rounds the share price to the nearest cent.

The prospectus sets separate limits on portfolio maturity. Dollar-weighted average maturity cannot exceed 60 calendar days, while dollar-weighted average life cannot exceed 120 calendar days under the prospectus's calculation rules. Managers normally hold securities to maturity. They may sell them when market or credit factors materially change.

Invesco already works with Superstate on the Invesco Short Duration US Government Securities Fund, represented by $USTB, which Invesco manages. According to the announcement, the new reserve fund is the first publicly registered fund on Superstate's FundOS platform. The platform now supports a publicly registered offering alongside private placements.

## Access, costs and redemption timing

The [product page](https://superstate.com/assets/iroxx) lists same-day subscriptions and redemptions with a 5pm ET cutoff and liquidity each market day. The fund distributes income daily. Before holding the tokenized shares, investors must register for a Superstate account, obtain approval and have their wallets added to the allowlist.

The gross expense ratio is 0.36%. A fee waiver and expense reimbursement reduce it to 0.20%. Invesco's contractual expense limit runs through December 31, 2027 unless extended. During that term, the fund's Board of Trustees must approve any increase in the limit or termination of the agreement. The cap excludes certain items described in the statement of additional information, as the prospectus notes.

Shares carry no sales charge. Blockchain transactions still incur costs outside the fund's expense ratio: shareholders pay gas for peer-to-peer transfers and redemptions. Invesco, Superstate or their affiliates pay the gas for purchases, including shares acquired through dividend reinvestment.

A shareholder can submit a redemption before the cutoff without the fund receiving it in good order before that cutoff. The product's risk disclosure warns that blockchain processing can delay receipt of the request and payment of proceeds. Until the next net asset value calculation, the shareholder remains exposed to changes in share value. Network congestion, insufficient gas or wallet disruption can interfere with processing.

The Bank of New York is the custodian and fund administrator. Invesco Advisers is the investment manager, Superstate Services is the sub-transfer agent and PriceWaterhouseCoopers is the listed auditor. The fund retains these conventional custody and administration arrangements alongside its blockchain recordkeeping.

## Ethereum shares with controlled transfers

A direct contract reading at Ethereum block 26,439,494 found [10,080,016.5 shares outstanding](https://etherscan.io/address/0x216099c3f392c870e47ead51ef9376a57bc77adf). The contract was deployed, reported six decimals and returned false for its paused flag. The reading measures token supply at that block. It does not measure the portfolio's holdings or the fund's capacity to meet redemptions.

The prospectus designates Ethereum as the fund's blockchain. On-chain ownership records and an off-chain register linking wallet addresses to shareholder identities together constitute the official shareholder register, subject to the prospectus's technical-failure qualification. Invesco Investment Services retains control over that recordkeeping through Superstate.

The network is public, but the shares are permissioned. Only registered, approved wallets can transact in them. Superstate's smart contracts support restrictions on transfers to or from unregistered wallets and the freezing of tokenized shares. They also support burning and re-minting shares when an investor loses control of a wallet. Balances are publicly visible, and the shares remain subject to those administrative powers.

Shareholders who lose a private key or have one stolen must contact Superstate promptly, according to the prospectus. Superstate's response can include investigating ownership, freezing the affected shares, revoking tokens and approving a replacement wallet. Where appropriate, it may facilitate payment for revoked shares by federal wire on a best-efforts basis. The prospectus still warns of irreversible losses if a third party uses a stolen key to transfer the shares and obtain the proceeds.

The fund expects stablecoin issuers to hold a significant portion of its shares. Events affecting an issuer, or changes in stablecoin redemptions, can trigger large purchases or withdrawals from the fund. Those flows can affect liquidity, expenses, yield and remaining shareholders. The fund's narrower range of eligible reserve assets may also result in a lower yield than money market funds with broader investment mandates.

The target share price is not guaranteed, and the investment has no federal deposit insurance. The sponsor has no obligation to reimburse losses or provide financial support during market stress.

Superstate's product page did not display a seven-day SEC yield or an unsubsidized yield when checked. Its DeFi integrations section was still marked "Coming soon", without a named live integration.
