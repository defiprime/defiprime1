A decentralized exchange settles trades through smart contracts, so orders match and clear without an operator taking custody of the assets. Designs range from constant-product pools to order books and curves tuned for assets that trade near parity, and aggregators route a single order across several of them.

The filters separate spot venues, aggregators, stable swaps, NFT marketplaces and the venues that ask for no identity check. Entries are ordered by 30-day trading volume on DefiLlama, with featured listings first. Aggregate [DEX volume](/dex-volume) is tracked separately.
