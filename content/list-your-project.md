The defiprime directory is curated. A listing costs **1000 USDC or USDT**, paid on-chain from your wallet at checkout. Paying does not guarantee a listing: if we decline, you get the full amount back.

How it works:

- Fill in the form, sign the submission with your wallet (free), then send the payment from that same wallet.
- We review every paid submission against the criteria on our [about page](/about.html) and reply by email.
- If we decline, we refund the full amount to the wallet that paid.
- An accepted listing is a one-time fee and stays in the directory for as long as the directory runs. Placement and ranking stay at our discretion.

Accepted payments are USDC and USDT on Ethereum and Arbitrum, and USDC on Base. Pay only through this page: funds sent on any other network or token may not be recoverable. You need a browser wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby.
